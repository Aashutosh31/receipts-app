# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for security problems.** Use GitHub
**private vulnerability reporting**: open this repository on GitHub, go to
the **Security** tab, and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Reports stay
private until a fix ships, and we will credit you if you wish.

Please include: what you found, where (file/route/version), steps to
reproduce, and what you believe the impact is. Do not exfiltrate other
users' data or disrupt the production Supabase project while testing —
use your own throwaway account.

## Response expectations

We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and to ship a fix or a
mitigation plan within 30 days, depending on severity. Critical findings
(exposed secrets, auth bypass, cross-user data leaks) are prioritized
above all other work.
